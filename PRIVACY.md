# FeedLens Privacy Policy

Last updated November 15, 2026

FeedLens helps you measure how much of your social media feed matches your interests and which posts you choose to expand.

## Data FeedLens uses

While monitoring is enabled for a supported social network, FeedLens reads text from posts that appear in your feed and detects clicks that expand a post. The text may include names or other information displayed in the post or its card. FeedLens stores post identifiers, dates and timestamps, expansion status, and classification results in Chrome's local extension storage. It also stores your Jev API key, interest names and descriptions, and extension preferences there. Full post text is not saved in the local history.

## Data sent to TypeSafe

FeedLens sends post text and your interest names and descriptions to TypeSafe's Jev API over HTTPS to classify posts. The request also sends your Jev API key to TypeSafe for authentication. TypeSafe processes these requests as a third-party service. Its handling of data is governed by the terms and privacy practices applicable to your TypeSafe account.

## Limited Use

FeedLens uses this data to provide the feed classification and activity reporting described above. FeedLens does not sell user data or use it for advertising, creditworthiness decisions, or lending. FeedLens's use and transfer of user data comply with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

## Your controls

You can turn off monitoring for any supported social network under **Settings → Monitored Networks**. To remove your saved API key, interests, preferences, and activity history from Chrome, choose **Settings → Delete All Data**. This action clears FeedLens's local storage. For questions about data already sent to TypeSafe, contact TypeSafe.

## Contact

For questions about this policy, use the developer contact information on the FeedLens Chrome Web Store listing.
